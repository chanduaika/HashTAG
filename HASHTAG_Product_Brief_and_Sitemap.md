# HASHTAG Product Brief and Sitemap

**Status:** Working product brief for implementation planning  
**Product:** HASHTAG laptops, ecommerce website, and Android app  
**Market:** India. Initial order availability is planned for Hyderabad and Visakhapatnam. Repair service starts in one city and expands to other major cities later; Hyderabad is the working assumption for the first service base.

## 1. Product vision

HASHTAG is an Indian laptop brand for students and working professionals, with a focus on developers and a separate gaming option. The proposed brand combines an affordable price range with a premium, youthful design, repairable hardware, and a transparent repair experience.

Customers should be able to discover and order laptops from either the website or Android app, then use the same account to see purchases, delivery progress, warranty information, and repair status.

## 2. Brand direction

- **Name/logo treatment:** `#HashTAG`
- **Personality:** Youthful, premium, approachable, and original.
- **Visual direction:** Dark graphite/navy foundation, warm white text, and electric teal accents.
- **Brand mark use:** A standalone `#` can appear on the laptop back; use the `#HashTAG` wordmark on the product and beside processor-brand logos as appropriate.

## 3. Customers and model families

### Student / Everyday

- General-purpose student laptops, with a 14-inch screen.
- Affordable entry configuration can start at 8 GB RAM.
- General-purpose configurations may offer 8 GB or 16 GB RAM and 512 GB storage.
- Example tiers: `#basic` (8 GB RAM / 256 GB storage, example Core i3) and `#median` (8 GB RAM / 512 GB storage, example Core i5).
- User-upgradeable RAM, with a target maximum of 32 GB.
- Battery should be replaceable by the customer, with accessible fasteners and clear instructions, to keep student repair costs down.
- Touchscreen is available only on selected student models.

### Working professional / Everyday

- General-purpose work laptops.
- Product page and catalog category should be distinct from student laptops.
- 8 GB or 16 GB RAM and 512 GB storage are the current general-purpose targets.
- Screen size may be 14 or 15.6 inches; configuration remains to be decided.

### Programming / Performance

- 15.6-inch model for programming and demanding work.
- Integrated graphics for the programming-focused configuration to support price and battery-life goals.
- 16/24/32 GB RAM options and 512 GB storage as a baseline, with up to 1 TB under consideration.
- Dedicated graphics are not required for this programming-focused configuration.
- Programming is a distinct integrated-graphics configuration, separate from gaming.
- Battery replacement should be handled by a technician rather than designed as a student self-repair task.
- Example tiers: `#power` (16 GB RAM / 512 GB storage, example Ryzen 7 + RTX 3050) and `#ultraPower` (16/24 GB RAM / 1 TB storage, example Ryzen 7 + RTX 3050/5050).
- Processor generation and GPU examples are concepts only, not final launch parts. Select supportable, current hardware after supplier research.

### Gaming

- A separate 15.6-inch gaming model.
- Dedicated graphics.
- 16/24/32 GB RAM options and 512 GB storage as a baseline, with up to 1 TB under consideration.
- Battery replacement should be handled by a technician rather than designed as a student self-repair task.

### Shared laptop goals

- Every laptop should have a 180-degree hinge.
- Touchscreen is optional and should vary by model.
- Spill resistance, backlit keyboard, and a premium design are desired. Repair costs should also be kept low.
- Target weight: 1.2–1.6 kg where feasible.
- Target battery life: at least 8 hours under typical use.
- Display direction: 14-inch student model; 15.6-inch programming and gaming models. IPS Full HD at 60 Hz is the baseline direction, with anti-glare and/or QHD options to be selected by model.
- Slim and light design; metal body is especially considered for models with dedicated graphics.
- Replaceable/upgradeable RAM and battery are core product principles; exact implementations can vary by model.

## 4. Sales and service policies

### Sales

- Website and Android app both support product discovery and ordering.
- One account works across both; account activity and customer data stay in sync.
- Customers register with both email and phone, verify both during initial sign-up, then create a password. Later logins use either email or phone plus password, without OTP each time. Both contact methods belong to one account.
- Customers choose from ready-made configurations; no custom configuration builder at launch.
- Current launch price target: ₹30,000–₹80,000 including taxes. Final model pricing is TBD.
- Proposed payment: 20% advance through UPI, with the remaining 80% at delivery by UPI or cash on delivery.
- Cancellation before packing: full refund, no cancellation charge.
- Cancellation after packing: customer can refuse/cancel at the doorstep; the tentative fee is 1% of the order total. Refund the advance after deducting the fee; confirm customer-facing policy before launch.
- Initial order availability is planned for Hyderabad and Visakhapatnam, with expansion to other cities later. Delivery partners, estimates, and shipping charges remain TBD.
- Arrival-fault replacement requires an unboxing video or delivery-person confirmation. The claim window is 48–72 hours after delivery; choose an exact cutoff before launch.
- Order updates should appear in the app only; no external SMS/email notifications are planned at launch.

### Warranty and repairs

- One-year warranty for eligible battery, screen, and motherboard faults.
- Warranty starts on the delivery date.
- Normal battery capacity loss over time is not covered; customer-caused damage may be chargeable.
- Technician provides an estimate first and proceeds only after customer approval.
- Target: technician contacts or visits within 24–48 hours where service is available; this is not a repair-completion guarantee.
- Start repair service from one major-city base (Hyderabad assumed), then add Visakhapatnam and other major-city bases as the network grows. Customers select the nearest base; the closest available trained technician within that base's coverage is assigned.
- Preferred at-home technician service where possible; courier service is another option. A physical store may be added later.
- Company sets fixed prices for common repair/service work. Parts and unusual repairs are quoted separately and require customer approval before work proceeds.
- Technician collects payment directly from the customer and remits the agreed commission to HASHTAG. Record the job, payment confirmation, technician, commission, and receipt against the service ticket.
- Customers should see clear repair status and relevant service details in their account.

## 7. Core customer journeys

### Purchase

Browse category → view model → choose ready-made configuration → sign in/verify → enter delivery details → pay 20% advance → receive order confirmation → track order → pay balance at delivery.

### Repair

Sign in → select a registered product → describe issue and raise complaint → see service request status → technician contacts customer / visit arranged → receive repair estimate → approve or decline → follow repair progress.

### Account sync

Customer uses verified email or phone to sign in on either website or Android app and sees the same orders, wishlist, registered products, warranty, and repair information.
