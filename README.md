# HASHTAG

HASHTAG is a proposed Indian laptop brand focused on repairability, accessible pricing, premium design, and transparent after-sales service.

This repository is the planning and implementation home for the HASHTAG ecommerce website and Android app. Both platforms are intended to share customer accounts, product catalog, orders, wishlist, warranty details, and repair records.

## Product direction

- **Customer groups:** Students, working professionals, programmers, and gamers.
- **Draft product tiers:** `#basic`, `#median`, `#power`, and `#ultraPower`.
- **Website:** Responsive ecommerce experience for India.
- **App:** Android first; the website serves iPhone users initially.
- **Initial order cities:** Hyderabad and Visakhapatnam.
- **Repair rollout:** Begin with one major-city base (Hyderabad assumed), then add other cities as trained technicians and operations are ready.
- **Design:** Youthful and premium, using a dark graphite/navy palette with electric teal accents. Logo concept is pending.

## Implementation direction

The planned system includes customer registration with email and phone verification, password login, product browsing, ready-made configurations, checkout, order tracking, repair requests, estimates and approvals, warranty information, and an administrative dashboard for staff.

Checkout and account verification should use test-mode behavior during development. Live payments and real email/SMS verification require the business accounts and providers to be configured. The customer-facing payment plan is 20% UPI advance and the remaining 80% at delivery by UPI or cash.
